## What X1 is

A high-performance **Layer 1 blockchain**, described as "a decentralised,
censorship-resistant multi-purpose infrastructure that empowers the freedom to
transact with minimal technical and economic limitations."

Fully compatible with the **Solana Virtual Machine (SVM)** — developers can
deploy Solana dApps on X1 without rebuilding their infrastructure.

## Signature features (good raw material for the "districts")

1. **Low-cost validator participation** — the standout claim: **"For just $5 a
   day, a validator can participate in the chain's consensus and earn rewards."**
   Zero-cost voting mechanism; far cheaper than typical competitors.
2. **Dynamic base fees** — congestion-reflective fees (conceptually like
   Ethereum's EIP-1559) instead of fixed rates. Prevents underpricing/spam via
   fair market pricing.
3. **SVM compatibility** — deploy without rebuilding; tap the Solana toolchain.
4. **Validator network** — **1,000+ validators** since mainnet launch.
5. **Monitoring** — live validator performance tracking at **x1val.online**.
